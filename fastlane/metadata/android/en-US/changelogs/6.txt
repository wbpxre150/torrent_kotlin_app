First public release.

- One APK that runs a DHT crawler, transmission-daemon, an FTP server and a
  cloudflared tunnel on the phone, supervised by a foreground service.
- On-device classifiers: CRAM v15 (category, TV kind and adult in one forward
  pass) and a llama.cpp movie-genre head, both downloaded and sha256-verified
  on first run instead of being bundled.
- Web dashboard with live logs, per-daemon control, search over the crawled
  index and one-tap add to transmission.
- All storage on a USB drive through the Storage Access Framework.
