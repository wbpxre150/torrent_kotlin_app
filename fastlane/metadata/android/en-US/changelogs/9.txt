No functional changes — a build release that makes the APK reproducible.

- The native daemons no longer embed absolute source paths. Every native build
  now passes -ffile-prefix-map, so libdhtcrawler.so and libtransmissiond.so are
  byte-identical no matter which directory the app is built in (previously
  they carried 192 and 56 build-machine paths respectively).
- Same app, same behaviour; the binaries are a few bytes smaller.
